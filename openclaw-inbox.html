OpenClaw Inbox Assistant

Private integration

OpenClaw Inbox Assistant is a private productivity integration operated by the owner of this site. It connects the owner’s Google account to a locally operated OpenClaw assistant for reviewing Gmail and managing Google Tasks.

What it does

The integration can read email selected for inbox review, apply user-approved mailbox changes such as labels, archive, or trash, and read or update the owner’s Google Tasks queue. It does not serve the public and does not provide access to other people’s Google accounts.

Email sending is disabled by default. The assistant may send or reply only after the account owner explicitly approves the exact recipient and final message.

Privacy

Google account data is used only to provide these owner-requested productivity functions. It is not used for advertising, sold, or shared with data brokers.

See the OpenClaw Inbox Assistant Privacy Policy for details about access, storage, retention, deletion, and revoking access.
