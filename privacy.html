Privacy

App version 89

FlipCast stores player names, games, scores, history, and statistics only in your browser on your device.

The app does not require an account and does not upload this game data to a server. The hosting provider may keep ordinary technical access logs when it delivers the app files.

Clearing this site’s browser data, uninstalling the app, or losing the device may permanently remove saved game data. Backup and transfer tools are not yet included in this beta.

When you choose to Cast, the live scoreboard is sent through Google Cast to the receiver you select. The public receiver does not retain the scoreboard.

If you choose Feedback, a Tally form opens with the app build, device platform, browser details, and whether you opened it from the installed app or a browser. Anything you enter or upload is submitted to Tally and is visible to the app owner. Your name and email are not required.

Anonymous beta analytics

FlipCast sends anonymous usage counts for app opens, games started and completed, selected edition, player and round counts, Cast attempts and connections, session duration, and recruitment campaign. It uses a random browser installation identifier so repeat use can be counted. The server stores only a salted hash of that identifier.

Analytics never includes player names, scores, game history, game identifiers, exact browser details, Cast diagnostics, or your IP address. Raw events are deleted after 90 days; aggregate counts may be retained.

Independent utility

This is an independent scorekeeping utility. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the publishers or trademark owners of any card game or beverage brand. All trademarks belong to their respective owners.
