OpenClaw Inbox Assistant Privacy Policy

September 13, 2026

This policy applies to OpenClaw Inbox Assistant, a private integration used only by its operator to connect the operator’s Google account to a locally operated OpenClaw assistant.

Google data accessed

With the account owner’s authorization, the integration may access Gmail messages, threads, labels, and mailbox state; basic Google account identity information; and Google Tasks lists and tasks. Access is limited to functions requested by the account owner.

How data is used

Google data is used to review and summarize inbox items, connect email with the owner’s task queue, and perform owner-approved Gmail or Google Tasks changes. Email sending is disabled by default and requires explicit approval of the exact recipient and final message.

Google user data is not used for advertising, profiling, or training public or shared artificial-intelligence models. It is not sold, rented, or shared with data brokers. The integration does not allow humans other than the account owner to read Google user data, except when required for security, legal compliance, or with the owner’s explicit consent.

Storage and protection

OAuth credentials and limited workflow metadata are stored on the operator-controlled computer in access-restricted credential and workspace files. Gmail content is retrieved only as needed for requested inbox work. This public website does not receive or store Google account data.

Retention and deletion

Workflow metadata is retained only as long as it remains useful to the owner and may be deleted from the local system at any time. Gmail and Google Tasks remain subject to Google’s own retention controls. The owner can delete or change those items in Google directly.

Sharing and transfers

Google user data is not transferred to third parties except to service providers necessary to operate the owner-requested assistant functions, under applicable privacy and security protections, or when legally required. Any use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

The account owner can revoke the integration at any time from Google Account third-party connections. Local credentials and retained workflow metadata can then be deleted from the operator-controlled computer.
