## Full description

FlipCast keeps game night moving with quick score entry, built-in card calculators, saved history, player statistics, and a live scoreboard for the TV.

Choose classic scoring or the With a Vengeance edition, add up to 18 players, and resume an interrupted game without creating an account. Scores, player names, history, and statistics stay in the app storage on your device.

Features:

- Fast round-by-round score entry
- Card calculators for Flip 7-style classic and With a Vengeance games
- Custom target scores
- Support for up to 18 players
- Game history and all-time player statistics
- Google Cast scoreboard for compatible TVs and streaming devices
- Offline scorekeeping after the first launch
- No account required
- Anonymous analytics can be disabled in Privacy settings

FlipCast is an independent scorekeeping utility. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the publishers or trademark owners of Flip 7 or any other card game. All trademarks belong to their respective owners.
