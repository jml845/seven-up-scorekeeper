# FlipCast player-led beta launch kit

## Outcome

Prove that independent Flip 7 groups can discover FlipCast, cast a game without coaching, and choose to use it again. The launch is successful when at least 10 separate groups complete 20 games, Cast works for at least 80% of compatible-device attempts, and no recurring scoring or game-state defect remains.

FlipCast remains a free, independent fan-made utility during this beta. Do not imply endorsement by Eric Olsen, The Op, or the Flip 7 trademark owners.

## IP guardrails for public promotion

Search results show a February 2026 r/boardgames thread titled **“Flip 7 score tracker app removed after copyright infringement complaint.”** The original post was later deleted. Surviving indexed comments indicate an unofficial Android app was removed from Google Play and that commenters focused on its use of `Flip 7` in the app name, but they do not identify the complainant, the complete listing, the assets it used, or the exact legal theory. Several other third-party Flip 7 scorekeepers remain publicly indexed. Treat this as a branding warning, not proof of a blanket prohibition on fan scorekeepers.

For the player-led web beta:

- confirm that FlipCast uses no publisher card scans, artwork, logos, rulebook text, sounds, or other copied creative assets;
- review the name, icon, game terminology, and marketing copy for trademark/confusion risk;
- describe FlipCast as an independent free fan-made utility and avoid implying official status;
- do not charge, run ads, solicit acquisition, or imply official compatibility/endorsement without a separate legal and business decision;
- retain the independent-project disclaimer, but do not treat a disclaimer as permission.

Written permission or legal review remains advisable before app-store distribution, monetization, paid promotion, or a publisher/acquisition pitch. It is not a prerequisite for a small, carefully worded web beta using original assets.

## Positioning

**One-line pitch:** A free Flip 7 scorekeeper that puts the live scoreboard, effects, and player standings on your TV.

**Short description:** Score from your phone while everyone at the table follows the game on a Chromecast-enabled TV. FlipCast supports classic Flip 7 and Flip 7: With a Vengeance, keeps game history and player statistics locally, and requires no account.

**Tester ask:** Try it for one game, then tell us whether the TV connected, whether scoring felt clear, and whether your group would use it again.

Public app: <https://jml845.github.io/seven-up-scorekeeper/>

## Staged rollout

### Stage 1: Ten privately invited groups

- Recruit friends, family, prior testers, and local game-night groups personally.
- Give each source a campaign link such as `?campaign=local_game_night`.
- Ask for one completed game, not a broad commitment to beta test.
- Stop and repair if the same Cast or scoring defect appears twice.

Gate to Stage 2:

- 10 groups attempted a game.
- At least five distinct phone/computer and TV combinations were exercised.
- No unresolved scoring defect.
- Cast succeeds for at least 8 of 10 compatible attempts, or the failures have a known fix.

### Stage 2: Community beta

Post one channel at a time, 24–48 hours apart, so feedback and failures can be attributed and handled without flooding the same build.

1. BoardGameGeek Flip 7 forum.
2. Relevant Facebook Flip 7 and board-game groups where self-promotion is allowed.
3. Relevant Discord playtest or digital-tool channels.
4. Reddit only in communities whose rules permit a free fan-project link.

Gate to Stage 3:

- 20 completed sessions from at least 10 independent groups.
- At least 80% compatible-device Cast success.
- At least 70% say they would use it again.
- Three quotable positive comments with explicit permission.

### Stage 3: Evidence-led outreach

- Publish a short demo with the measured beta results and selected comments.
- Recontact Eric Olsen once with evidence, not another testing obligation.
- If use is material, ask The Op about permission, collaboration, licensing, or acquisition before monetization.
